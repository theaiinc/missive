Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

Missive is an email and calendar app operated by The AI Inc ("we", "us"). It's available at missive.theaiinc.com and at addresses of organizations that use it, such as mail.bugmole.com. This policy explains what data Missive handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions: info@theaiinc.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and organization. These come from Aegis, our sign-in service at id.theaiinc.com, when you sign in.
  • Mail in hosted mailboxes: messages sent to and from addresses Missive hosts for your organization.
  • Accounts you connect: if you connect a Gmail, Outlook or other IMAP account, the sign-in tokens (or IMAP password) for it, and the mail and calendars you let Missive read from it.
  • What you create: folders, rules, calendars, events, and files you import, such as .ics calendars and links to them.
  • Technical records: sync logs and error records needed to run and fix the service. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies in Missive. The only cookies are for keeping you signed in.

How we use it

We use your data only to provide Missive's features to you:

  • showing your mail and calendars;
  • sending mail you write;
  • applying the rules and folders you set up;
  • delivering mail to your organization's shared addresses;
  • keeping the service secure and working.

We don't sell your data. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't use it to train AI models. Where Missive offers AI features, such as sorting mail, they run on a model we operate ourselves or on a provider processing data on our behalf under the same restrictions. They only produce results for you.

Google user data

If you connect a Google account, Missive requests these permissions:

  • Gmail (gmail.readonly, gmail.modify, gmail.send): to show that account's mail in Missive, to apply the labels and organizing you choose, and to send mail you write from that account.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly): to show that account's calendars and events in Missive's calendar, read-only. Missive never changes your Google Calendar.
  • Your email address (userinfo.email): to know which account you connected.

Missive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • We use Google user data only to provide and improve the features described above, which you can see in Missive.
  • We don't transfer it to others except to provide those features, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • We don't use it for advertising.
  • We don't use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person reads it unless you ask us to (for example for support), it's needed for security or to comply with the law, or it has been made unidentifiable.

The same limits apply to data from Microsoft accounts you connect (Mail.Read, Mail.Send, Calendars.Read and related permissions).

Where it's stored and how it's protected

  • Missive runs on Cloudflare, and its database is hosted by Neon (PostgreSQL) in the United States.
  • Mail content, calendar details (titles, notes, places, guests, links) and connected-account tokens are encrypted before they're stored, with keys held separately from the database.
  • Everything is kept per person: each person's data is only readable by queries made for that person.
  • Traffic is encrypted with TLS.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • Your data is kept while you have a Missive account.
  • Disconnecting an account in Missive's settings immediately deletes its stored tokens and the calendars copied from it, and Missive stops reading it. Mail already copied from that account stays in your Missive mailbox until you delete it.
  • To have all your data deleted, including mail copied from connected accounts, email info@theaiinc.com from your account's address. We delete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something.
  • You can also remove Missive's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

We share data only with the service providers that run Missive for us: Cloudflare for hosting, delivery and email routing, and Neon for the database. They process it on our instructions. We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

Within your organization:

  • Mail sent to a shared group address is delivered to that group's members.
  • Your organization's Missive administrators can manage mailboxes and groups on its domains.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it's used, by emailing info@theaiinc.com. Missive isn't meant for children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If the change is significant, we'll tell you in Missive or by email before it takes effect.
