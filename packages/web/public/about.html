Your email and calendar, in one private place

Missive is an email and calendar app made by The AI Inc. It hosts mailboxes on your organization's own domain, shares team addresses like hello@ between the people who answer them, and brings in the accounts you already use.

What it does

  • Hosted mailboxes on your domain, with groups that deliver to several people.
  • Connected accounts: read and send the mail of a Gmail or Outlook account you connect, alongside your hosted mail.
  • Calendar: your own calendars, .ics files and calendar links you import, and a read-only view of the calendars of a Google or Microsoft account you connect.
  • Organizing: rules and folders that sort incoming mail for you.

How it uses your Google account

If you choose to connect a Google account, Missive asks for permission to read, send and organize that account's Gmail and to read its calendars. It uses that access only to show that mail and those calendars to you in Missive, to send mail you write, and to apply the organizing you set up. See the privacy policy for details, including how Missive follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.
